name=Utility Network Designer (graph engine)
qgisMinimumVersion=3.28
description=Designs water, electricity and gas distribution networks on the plane street graph of a district, validates every design rule, and analyses reliability, crossings and depths.
about=Processing algorithms around the graph-theoretic engine of the paper series on utility networks for Gombe (Kinshasa): (1) design the three networks from streets, buildings and the district boundary, with every rule of the rule packs checked as a property of plane graphs; (2) analyse the reliability of the water network (valve segments, N-1, district metered areas, fire flow), of the electricity network (LV ties, MV open rings, trench cuts) and the vertical design of the corridor; (3) draw longitudinal profiles, cross-sections, junction plans and single-line diagrams. Requires numpy, scipy, pandas, shapely, geopandas, networkx and matplotlib: the QGIS installers for Windows ship them; on Linux and macOS install them in the Python of QGIS. Licence: MIT.
